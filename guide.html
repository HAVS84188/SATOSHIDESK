📘 Welcome to SatoshiDesk: The Complete Beginner's Guide

SatoshiDesk is a full-stack, in-browser Bitcoin Trading Terminal, Autonomous Algorithmic Bot Sandbox, SHA-256 Blockchain Settlement Engine, and AI Quantitative Analyst built from scratch using 100% Pure Vanilla JavaScript (Zero Frameworks). Whether you are new to crypto trading or evaluating this project's software architecture, this page explains everything shown on the screen.

1. Reading the Live Candlestick Chart (HTML5 Canvas)

Instead of using third-party chart libraries, SatoshiDesk renders its own 60FPS candlestick chart using 2D Canvas coordinate mathematics.

  • Green Candle (Bullish): The price closed higher than it opened during that time interval.
  • Red Candle (Bearish): The price closed lower than it opened.
  • Wicks (Thin Vertical Lines): Show the highest and lowest price reached during that candle.
  • SMA-7 (Cyan Line) & SMA-25 (Gold Line): Simple Moving Averages smooth out price noise. When the fast Cyan line crosses above the slow Gold line, traders call it a Bullish Crossover.

2. How the $100,000 Paper Trading Desk Works

You start with $100,000 in virtual USDT (Tether, pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar) so you can practice trading real-time Bitcoin price movements with zero financial risk.

  • Market Order: Buys or sells Bitcoin immediately at the current live market price.
  • Limit Order: Lets you set a target price (e.g., Buy if BTC drops to $84,000). SatoshiDesk's JavaScript order-matching engine monitors every live tick and fills your order automatically the millisecond the price crosses your target.
  • Portfolio Equity: Automatically calculates your total net worth: Available USDT + (BTC Holdings × Live BTC Price).

3. RSI (14) & The Autonomous Trading Bot

In the top ticker bar, you will see RSI (14)—the Relative Strength Index, a momentum oscillator ranging from 0 to 100.

  • RSI Below 32 (Oversold): Indicates sellers are exhausted and the price may bounce upward.
  • RSI Above 68 (Overbought): Indicates buyers are overheated and the price may cool down.
  • Autonomous RSI Scalper Bot: When you check the bot toggle in the terminal, JavaScript automatically buys 0.10 BTC when RSI drops below 32 and sells when RSI climbs above 68!

4. Whale Radar & Web Audio Synthesizer

In financial markets, a "Whale" is an institutional trader or wealthy entity executing massive orders capable of moving the market price.

  • Live Market Tape: Streams real-time trades on the right side of the terminal.
  • Whale Detection (>$50,000 USD): Whenever a single trade exceeds $50,000 in value (or when you click the "Simulate $250K Whale Spike" button), the trade highlights in gold and triggers a synthesized sonar ping using the browser's native Web Audio API (zero MP3 files required).

5. SHA-256 Blockchain & Proof-of-Work Mining

How does Bitcoin verify transactions without a central bank? Tab 2 demonstrates a real cryptographic blockchain ledger.

  • Unconfirmed Mempool: Every trade you make in Tab 1 is placed into a waiting room called the Mempool.
  • Proof-of-Work (PoW) Mining: Clicking Mine Pending Block bundles your mempool trades into a block and runs a SHA-256 hashing loop, incrementing a number called a Nonce thousands of times until it discovers a cryptographic hash starting with three zeros (000...).
  • Tamper Detection: Every block stores the hash of the block before it. If you click "Tamper With Block Data", the block's SHA-256 signature changes, immediately breaking the chain and alerting the network!

6. Software Engineering Highlights (Under the Hood)

Built specifically for high performance and low memory consumption:

  • Custom Pub/Sub Reactive Store: Unidirectional state management built without React or Redux.
  • Hybrid Resilient Market Stream: Connects to Binance's live WebSocket (wss://stream.binance.com) and automatically falls back to a synthetic Monte-Carlo tick generator if your firewall or campus Wi-Fi blocks crypto streams.
  • DeepSeek Cloud + Deterministic Quant Engine: Supports live LLM telemetry analysis via the DeepSeek API, plus an offline mathematical rule engine that works immediately with zero API keys.
